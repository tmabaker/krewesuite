KreweWorks

Every Hire, Change and Exit, Handled.

Paper checklists get lost. Email chains between managers, HR and IT stall a new hire before day one. KreweWorks gives every person one record and every request one route, so the account, the laptop and the paperwork are ready when your new team member walks in.

See How It Works
Features

One Route for Every Employee Event

Built with HR teams and hiring managers who were tired of chasing paperwork by email.

Hire

The hiring manager starts the request, HR screens and approves, and the manager sets the start date and the IT checklist. HR sends one New Hire Notice and IT builds everything before day one.

Change

Transfers, promotions, new managers, new desks and new access all follow the same route. HR approves, IT applies, the manager confirms. Nothing gets lost between departments.

Terminate

HR and IT hear the moment a departure is filed. Involuntary exits are flagged urgent so access ends on time, equipment comes back and every system is closed out.

Confidential Hires

Mark a hire confidential and only HR, the hiring manager and whoever filed it can see it until IT needs it. A duplicate check never reveals who is in the pipeline.
