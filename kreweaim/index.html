KreweAIM

AI Mastery for Every Member.

Most companies buy AI seats and hope people figure them out. Few do. KreweAIM gives each member a personal program for the AI tools their company provides, shaped by the work they do, how skilled they are and where AI has let them down before. Every person learns on their own path. The coaching comes to them, and it never goes stale.

See How It Works
Features

AI Skills That Pay for the License

Built by an MSP that watched expensive AI seats sit idle.

Assigned by IT

Your IT admin or NOIT enrolls people with one security group. Members get a fleur de lis beside their name in KreweConnect.

Only Your Tools

Lessons cover the AI platforms your company actually licenses and approves. Nobody is trained on a tool they cannot open, and your own AI policy comes first.

Coached to the Person

Role, skill and real usage decide what comes next. When something goes wrong, the next lesson is about exactly that, and access problems go straight to IT.

Never Finished

AI tools change every month. New features and releases roll into the program as they ship, and people get them before they think to go looking.

How It Works

From Enrolled to Fluent

1

Enroll

IT adds the person to the KreweAIM group and the program builds itself from their role and tools

2

3

Keep Getting Better

Usage, feedback and new releases reshape the program, and leaders see the progress

Now in Pilot

Better Together

Measured, Not Guessed

Safe First
